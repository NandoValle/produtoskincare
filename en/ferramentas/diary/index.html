Skin diary: your progress, made visible

Log how your skin is feeling, add photos whenever you like — and see your progress curve over time. Every record stays on this device (the browser's local storage); nothing is sent to the site.

Skin changes in cycles, not days: epidermal renewal takes about 28 days, and actives like retinol usually show visible difference after 8 to 12 weeks. Shoot the same way every time (same hour, diffuse window light, same angle, clean face). A personal record — not a clinical measurement.
