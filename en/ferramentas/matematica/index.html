The math of the bottle

The sticker price is what you pay today. What decides whether skincare fits the budget is what it costs per day: the same bottle can be cheap or expensive depending on how much you put on your face. Here the math is done in the open, in two columns: the dose most people apply and the right dose.

How it works

  • The math is the same as the Refill reminder: size divided by how much you use per day gives the days it lasts; the price divided by those days gives the cost per day.
  • The doses are average estimates. Adjust to the way you actually use it.
  • Every result comes in two columns: the dose most people apply (about half the recommended one) and the right dose.
  • What you type stays on your device. No sign-up.

The lesson that changes the price of everything: the right dose

Application studies usually show people use far less product than the recommended dose. On the label, the bottle seems to last months; at the right dose, it runs out halfway through. It is not that the product got expensive — before, there was product missing from your face.

An example with the assumptions in plain sight

Real price: Neutrogena Sun Fresh Derm Care FPS 70 at R$ 47.70, checked on 02/11/2026 (site catalog). For the math, assume a 50 ml bottle used once a day — a common market size, but check your own label.

Sunscreen is where it hurts most: it is the product that suffers most from underdosing, and the only one whose job depends on how much you apply. Using half the SPF is not saving — it is less protection for the same price.

    In short

    • The sticker price says little. The cost per day and how long it lasts say whether the product fits your budget.
    • The dose most people apply makes the bottle look like it lasts twice as long and costs half as much. At the right dose, the math changes.
    • Sunscreen deserves the full dose: underdosing saves nothing and protects less.
    • Dose and size estimates are averages. The numbers that count are the ones on your label and in your own habits.
